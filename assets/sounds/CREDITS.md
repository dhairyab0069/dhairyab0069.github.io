# Sound credits

The touch sound effects on the site are short clips cut from two CC0 packs. CC0 is a
public-domain dedication, so no credit is required, but credit is given anyway.

| Clip | Used for | Source file | Pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tap.mp3` | taps on filters, steps, tags | `tick_001` | Interface Sounds by Kenney |
| `toggle.mp3` | the Motion and Cheat code buttons | `toggle_002` | Interface Sounds by Kenney |
| `on.mp3` | turning sound on | `confirmation_002` | Interface Sounds by Kenney |
| `off.mp3` | turning sound off | `close_002` | Interface Sounds by Kenney |
| `strike.mp3` | link and explore buttons (a blade ting) | `impactMetal_light_001` | Impact Sounds by Kenney |
| `theme-b.mp3` | the end of the theme switch (a metal hit) | `impactMetal_medium_001` | Impact Sounds by Kenney |
| `close.mp3` | closing a Journey item (a metal latch) | `metalLatch` | RPG Audio by Kenney |
| `nav.mp3` | changing page (a swoosh) | `swing` | RPG Sound Pack by artisticdude |
| `open.mp3` | opening a Journey item (a blade draw) | `sword-unsheathe` | RPG Sound Pack by artisticdude |
| `theme-a.mp3` | the start of the theme switch (a magic charge) | `magic1` | RPG Sound Pack by artisticdude |

- Kenney: https://kenney.nl (Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio), CC0,
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- artisticdude: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack (CC0). The author asks for
  credit to "artisticdude", with a link back to OpenGameArt.

Edits made here: the start silence was trimmed, each clip was cut to a short length with a
40 to 60 ms fade-out, converted to mono, normalized to a peak of about -3 dB and encoded as
80 kbps MP3. The original files are unchanged at their sources.
